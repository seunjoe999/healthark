About CompCare Hub

The platform that puts
carers first

At CompCare Hub, we believe that great care starts with empowered carers. We built this platform specifically for UK care homes — to remove the administrative burden that too often takes time and energy away from the people who need care.

Whether you manage a single residential home or a network of care facilities, CompCare Hub unifies everything — resident records, medication management, staff rotas, CQC compliance, and recruitment — into one beautifully simple system that your whole team can use from day one.

For Our Caregivers

The people behind
exceptional care

Care workers are the heart of every care home. They build relationships, provide comfort, and make a profound difference in people's lives — often in the most challenging of circumstances.

CompCare Hub is designed to support and celebrate your care team — reducing their paperwork load, giving them clear guidance, and letting them get on with what they do best.

  • 1
    Care Assistants & Support Workers
    Log daily records, check care plans, record observations and complete handovers — all from a phone or tablet in seconds.
  • 2

    Manage MAR charts, document clinical observations, coordinate medication rounds, and access complete resident histories at a glance.
  • 3
    Senior Staff & Managers
    Oversee rotas, review incident reports, manage compliance, run reports, and keep every team member informed in real time.
Core Features

Everything your care home needs,
nothing you don't

CompCare Hub replaces clipboards, spreadsheets, and disconnected software with one unified platform your whole team will actually use.

🏥
Resident Management

Complete digital profiles for every resident — personal history, care needs, preferences, family contacts, and photo ID. All accessible 24/7 from any device.

📋
Person-Centred Care Plans

Create, review, and update care plans that truly reflect each individual. Set goals, track outcomes, and share updates with the full care team instantly.

💊
Digital MAR Charts

Safe, accurate medication management. Record administrations, flag missed doses, manage stock levels, and maintain a complete audit trail for every resident.

📅
Rota & Shift Management

Build weekly and monthly rotas visually. Set minimum staffing levels, spot conflicts automatically, and keep your team informed of changes in real time.

⏰
QR Clock In / Out

Staff scan a unique QR code at the start and end of every shift. Timesheets are generated automatically — no more paper signing-in books.

🚨
Safeguarding & Incidents

Log incidents, near-misses, and safeguarding concerns with guided workflows. Every report is date-stamped, linked to the resident, and ready for CQC review.

📊
Real-Time Reporting

From occupancy rates to medication compliance — generate professional reports instantly. Data that helps you make better decisions for your home.

🏖️
Leave & HR Management

Staff submit holiday requests digitally. Managers approve or decline with one tap. Running holiday totals, entitlements, and HR notes all in one place.

💬
Team Messaging & Alerts

Secure internal messages, broadcast announcements, and instant notifications for medication times, incidents, or urgent updates — all delivered in-app.

A Carer's Day

How CompCare fits into
your team's day

From the morning handover to the end-of-shift sign-off, CompCare Hub is there at every step — making every task faster, clearer, and better documented.

07:00 — Morning
☀️
Handover & Briefing

Night staff log their handover notes. Day team reviews alerts, care plan changes, and today's medication schedule — all from one screen.

09:30 — Morning Round
💊
Medication Round

Nurses confirm each administration on the digital MAR. Any refusals or concerns are flagged immediately and routed to the senior on duty.

14:00 — Afternoon
📝
Daily Records & Observations

Care workers log meals, mood, mobility, and wellbeing notes for each resident on their tablets — no paper, no transcription, no lost records.

19:00 — Evening
📋
End-of-Shift Sign Off

Staff clock out with QR codes. Managers review the day's summary, flag anything for night team, and tomorrow's rota is already confirmed.

CQC & GDPR

Compliance built in,
not bolted on

CompCare Hub was designed around the CQC's five key questions — Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive, and Well-Led. Every feature creates evidence that supports your inspection rating.

Equally, every piece of resident and staff data is stored securely, encrypted at rest and in transit, and fully aligned with UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 requirements — giving you, your residents, and their families complete peace of mind.
