Core Features

Everything your care home needs

Purpose-built for UK care providers — replacing paper trails and fragmented systems with one secure, compliant platform your whole team will love.

Resident Management

Full resident profiles, care plans, risk assessments and daily records — all accessible to authorised staff 24/7 from any device.

CQC Compliance

Safeguarding logs, incident reports, and audit trails mapped to CQC's five key questions. Always inspection-ready — no frantic paperwork.
